Common questions

How do I unlock the next lesson?
Finish the lesson before it. Each section has its own path, so you can switch sections any time from the top of the Learn screen.

Where are my saved verses?
Open the Saved tab. You can add notes, search, copy, and practice them.

How do I change or stop daily reminders?
Go to Profile, then Daily reminder. You can also manage notifications in iPhone Settings, then Scroll.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on your device and included in your iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Which translation does Scroll use?
The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a modern, accurate, public-domain translation.
